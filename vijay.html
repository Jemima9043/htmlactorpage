In 1997, Vijay appeared in Kaalamellam Kaathiruppen, Love Today and Once More, and in the latter he shared the screen with veteran actor Sivaji Ganesan. In Nerrukku Ner, he starred with actor Suriya's marked debut in the industry. Then, Vijay attracted the family audience through Fazil's Kadhalukku Mariyadhai.
